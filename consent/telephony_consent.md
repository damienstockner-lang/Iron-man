# Telephony & Recording Consent

This app may provide features that place automated calls or record audio for voicemails and call transcription. These capabilities require explicit user consent.

By enabling telephony features you confirm the following:

- You are the owner of the phone number you register, or you have explicit permission from the owner to register it.
- You consent to sending and receiving SMS messages for verification and notifications.
- You consent to the app initiating outbound calls and, when explicitly requested, playing synthesized speech (TTS) during a call to speak on your behalf.
- You consent to the recording and storage of voicemails and call audio when the "Record Voicemail" feature is enabled. Recorded audio will be stored encrypted and retained only for the period you specify in settings.
- You will obtain consent from third parties before recording calls where required by law. The app will present a clear disclosure and require your confirmation before recording.

Privacy and data handling
- Phone numbers and PII will be stored encrypted at rest.
- Telephony provider logs (Twilio) may exist outside this app; review the provider's privacy policy.
- You may delete your stored voicemails and messages at any time from the app.

If you do not agree to these terms, disable telephony features and do not verify a phone number.
